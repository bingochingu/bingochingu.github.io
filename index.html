About Me

I'm a third-year Financial Analysis and Financial Technology student with a growing passion for quantitative trading and community engagement.

"Research is not just about discovery — it's about creating knowledge that transforms lives."

Over my first two years at university, I worked with NGOs and launched projects supporting low-income families in Hong Kong. I also secured seed funding in a start-up competition and interned at a large company where I helped build software that streamlined workflows and improved operational efficiency.

Above all, I aspire to become a professor. I see teaching and research as inseparable and my goal is to educate the next generation, mentor students, and build a career dedicated to both academic inquiry and meaningful community impact.

Research & Academic Interests

AI and Finance

My research interests lie at the intersection of finance and technology, particularly the disruptive role of AI in reshaping traditional finance, from algorithmic trading to risk modelling and fraud detection.

Financial Literacy

Beyond the technical side, I aim to advance public financial literacy through data-driven research, making finance more accessible to everyday people.

Academic Vision

As I work toward becoming a professor, my goal is to build a research agenda that bridges quantitative finance, AI, and public education, advancing knowledge while making it useful to society.
